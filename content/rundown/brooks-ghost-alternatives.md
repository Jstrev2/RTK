**Short answer:** The Mizuno Wave Rider 30 is the closest Brooks Ghost 18 alternative: $145, 9.8 oz and the same 12 mm drop. The Saucony Ride 19 is lighter at 9.3 oz.

## The Ghost 18 is the benchmark

The [Ghost 18](/shoes/brooks-ghost-18) is $150 and 9.8 oz with a 36 mm stack and a 12 mm drop. Our data lists it as dependable, durable and a fit for most feet, with wide sizes. The weaknesses are little energy return and a firmer ride than max-cushion shoes.

## Same drop and same job

The [Mizuno Wave Rider 30](/shoes/mizuno-wave-rider-30) is $145 and 9.8 oz with a 38 mm stack and a 12 mm drop. It has a stable, structured ride and is built for high mileage. It is not soft enough for runners who want a plush recovery shoe.

The Nike Pegasus 42 is $145 and 9.8 oz with a 37 mm stack and a 10 mm drop. See our [Pegasus alternatives guide](/rundown/nike-pegasus-alternatives) for its own rivals.

## Lighter with an 8 mm drop

- [Saucony Ride 19](/shoes/saucony-ride-19): $145, 9.3 oz, 35 mm stack. Balanced cushion and a durable upper.
- [ASICS Gel-Cumulus 28](/shoes/asics-gel-cumulus-28): $145, 9.2 oz, 38 mm stack. Light for its cushion with an accommodating fit.
- New Balance Fresh Foam X 880 v15: $140, 10.4 oz, 36 mm stack. Heavier but available in several widths.

Moving from 12 mm to 8 mm is a small change. Our [heel drop guide](/rundown/heel-drop-explained-4mm-vs-10mm) covers how to ramp up if your calves feel it.

## Cheaper or softer within the same brand

- Brooks Revel 9: $100, 8.7 oz, 33 mm stack, 8 mm drop. Firm and basic, good for short daily runs.
- Brooks Glycerin 23: $165, 10.1 oz, 38 mm stack, 10 mm drop. The plush Brooks.
- Brooks Ghost Max 4: $160, 10.3 oz, 39 mm stack, 6 mm drop. More protection, too heavy for workouts.

## If the Ghost is the wrong shape

Runners who overpronate and want built-in support should read our [stability vs neutral guide](/rundown/stability-shoes-vs-neutral-do-you-need-support). The Adrenaline GTS 26 is $155 and 10.8 oz, the support version in the same brand.

Runners who want a softer, taller ride should look at the Hoka Clifton 11 instead. The [Shoe Finder](/tools/shoe-selector) narrows the list by fit and cushion.
