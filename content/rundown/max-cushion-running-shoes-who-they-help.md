**Short answer:** Buy a max cushion shoe for easy and long runs, not for speed. A 2018 lab study found no running economy gain from the category, and our data shows it costs weight and money.

## Max cushion is now the biggest category in our data

Our shoe database lists 155 of 339 models as maximum cushion. Their median stack is 40 mm, against 36 mm for moderate shoes and 30 mm for minimal ones.

The median max cushion shoe is $180 and 9.6 oz (men's). Moderate shoes are $145 and 9.2 oz. You pay about $35 and 0.4 oz for the extra foam.

## The tall stack is a poor fit for faster running

Our data lists the same weakness again and again. The [Nimbus 27](/shoes/asics-gel-nimbus-27) is "too soft and slow-feeling for workouts", and the [Vomero 18](/shoes/nike-vomero-18) is "too much shoe for speed work".

Some are light. 53 of the 155 max cushion shoes weigh under 9 oz, and the [Novablast 6](/shoes/asics-novablast-6) is $150 and 9 oz with a 41 mm stack. The soft, tall platform still favors steady running.

## Research shows comfort, not speed

Mercer, Stone, Young and Mercer (2018, International Journal of Exercise Science) tested a maximal cushioning shoe against a neutral shoe. Running economy did not change. It is one lab study, so treat it as an absence of proof of a benefit, not proof of harm.

The reason to buy is how the shoe feels at mile 10, not what it does to your pace.

## The default pick is the Novablast 6

The Novablast 6 is the pick for most runners who want max cushion. It is $150, 9 oz and 8 mm drop, and our data calls it light for the cushion on offer.

Its weakness is stability. If tall soft shoes feel wobbly on corners, the [Brooks Ghost Max 4](/shoes/brooks-ghost-max-4) has a wider base at $160, 10.3 oz and 6 mm drop. Browse the full [max cushion shoe list](/shoes/best/max-cushion-running-shoes) for more.

## Who max cushion is wrong for

- Runners who do most of their miles at tempo pace or faster. A moderate-stack trainer from the [shoe selector](/tools/shoe-selector) fits better.
- Runners who want ground feel. Only 12 shoes in our data are minimal cushion.
- Runners who want one shoe to last. Soft foam wears down, and our [shoe lifespan guide](/rundown/how-long-do-running-shoes-last) has the replacement signs.

If you already own a firmer daily trainer, a max cushion pair makes a good second shoe in a [two-shoe rotation](/rundown/shoe-rotation-two-pairs).
