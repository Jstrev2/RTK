**Short answer:** Nathan Martin, a substitute teacher and high school coach from Jackson, Michigan, won the 2026 Los Angeles Marathon by 0.01 seconds. Priscah Cherono, a 45-year-old mother of three, won the women's race and the Marathon Chase bonus.

## The men's race was decided by a hundredth of a second

- Winner: Nathan Martin, USA, 2:11:16.50
- Second: Michael Kamau, Kenya, the same official time to the second
- Margin: 0.01 seconds, the closest finish in the race's history

Kamau led late in the race. Martin closed on him over the final stretch and passed him in the last strides before the line in Century City. Kamau collapsed just after the finish and was taken off on a stretcher. Reports said he had not taken fluids during the race.

Some outlets list Martin's time as 2:11:17 or 2:11:18. The race's own awards page gives 2:11:16.50.

## Martin coaches teenagers between races

Martin works as a substitute teacher and coaches cross-country at Jackson High School. He ran for Spring Arbor University and qualified for the U.S. Olympic Trials in 2016 and 2020. Coverage of the race called him the fastest U.S.-born Black marathoner.

He is the second American man in a row to win in Los Angeles, after Matthew Richtman in 2025.

## Cherono led from mile 1 and beat the men to the tape

- Winner: Priscah Cherono, Kenya, 2:25:20 official
- Second: Kellyn Taylor, USA, 2:27:37
- Gap: 2 minutes 17 seconds

Cherono, 45, lives in Colorado Springs and placed 10th in the 5,000 meters at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. She led from mile 1 to the finish.

Los Angeles runs a "Marathon Chase": the elite women start ahead of the elite men, and the first runner across the line wins a $10,000 bonus. Cherono crossed before any man.

## What it means for the rest of the field

More than 27,000 runners ran from Dodger Stadium to Century City. Kamau's collapse after a race without fluids is the reminder for everyone else: drink and fuel on a schedule, even when you feel fine.
