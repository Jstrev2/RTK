**Short answer:** Start with the Altra Torin 9 at $150, or the Saucony Echelon 11 at $155 if your feet are very wide. Our data lists only 39 of 339 shoes with a wide or extra-wide toe box.

## Roomy toe boxes are rare

Of 339 shoes in our database, 44 are narrow and 256 are standard. Only 34 are wide and 5 are extra wide.

Altra makes 16 of the 39. Topo Athletic has 7, Brooks 5 and Saucony 4. The rest are Mount to Coast, New Balance, Craft, Hoka and Reebok.

## The Torin 9 is the default pick

The [Altra Torin 9](/shoes/altra-torin-9) is $150 and 10.1 oz with a 30 mm stack and zero drop. Our data calls it the safest recommendation for runners moving into low-drop shoes who still want real cushioning.

Zero drop is the catch. If your calves are used to a 10 mm shoe, read the [heel drop guide](/rundown/heel-drop-explained-4mm-vs-10mm) before buying, or take the Altra Experience Flow 3 at $145 with a 4 mm drop.

## Models by what you need

- Widest fit: [Saucony Echelon 11](/shoes/saucony-echelon-11), extra wide, $155, 11.5 oz, 8 mm drop, built for wide feet and custom orthotics.
- Max cushion with a toe box: [Topo Athletic Phantom 4](/shoes/topo-athletic-phantom-4), $150, 9.5 oz, 35 mm stack, 5 mm drop.
- Light stability: [Hoka Arahi 9](/shoes/hoka-arahi-9), $150, 9.7 oz, wider forefoot than the Arahi 8.
- Fast days: Topo Athletic Cyclone 3, $150, 6.9 oz, no plate.

## Check the fit in the shop

One 2024 randomized crossover study in Bioengineering gave 25 male marathon runners raised toe box shoes and regular shoes. Its authors concluded the raised toe box reduced toe injuries from long-distance running. It is a small study of men only.

A common fit check is a thumbnail's width between your longest toe and the shoe's end. Your toes should sit clear of the sides after 30 minutes of running.

## Who these are wrong for

- Runners with narrow heels. A roomy last can slip, so use the lacing to lock the heel.
- Racers who want a carbon plate. The [Altra Vanish Carbon 3](/shoes/altra-vanish-carbon-3) is $240, and plated wide options are rare.
- Anyone who needs support. Filter the [wide toe box shoe list](/shoes/best/wide-toe-box-running-shoes) by stability.
