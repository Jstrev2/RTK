**Short answer:** Buy a cushioned daily trainer, not a racer. The [Hoka Clifton 11](/shoes/hoka-clifton-11) is 50 and 9.7 oz with a 42 mm stack, and our data notes it works for walking and standing all day.

## Pick a daily trainer with a 36 to 43 mm stack

A daily trainer has a soft midsole, a durable outsole and a roomy fit. All-day walking asks for the same things, only with more hours on pavement and less speed. These four fit that description in our data:

- [Hoka Clifton 11](/shoes/hoka-clifton-11): 50, 9.7 oz, 42 mm stack, 8 mm drop.
- [Brooks Ghost 18](/shoes/brooks-ghost-18): 50, 9.8 oz, 36 mm stack, 12 mm drop. Wide sizes are available.
- [Saucony Triumph 24](/shoes/saucony-triumph-24): 70, 9.7 oz, 37 mm stack, 10 mm drop.
- [Hoka Bondi 9](/shoes/hoka-bondi-9): 70, 10.6 oz, 43 mm stack, 5 mm drop, with a wide, stable base.

## The Clifton 11 is the default

It has the highest stack after the Bondi 9 at a lower price, and our data lists smooth rockered transitions. The cons in our data are a ride that gets sluggish at faster paces and ordinary foam for 50. Those matter for a runner and barely matter for a walker.

The Ghost 18 is the pick if you want a firmer, more familiar feel or need a wide size. The Bondi 9 suits heavier walkers who want the most protection.

## Who should skip these

- Anyone who wants one shoe for fast running too. A softer trainer is slow at tempo pace. The [shoe selector](/tools/shoe-selector) narrows by run type.
- Walkers with narrow feet. The Triumph 24 and Clifton 11 fit a standard width, so try them on.
- Anyone on a tight budget. The [good running shoes under $120](/rundown/good-running-shoes-under-120) guide lists cheaper options.

## What to skip

Skip carbon-plated racers and minimal speed shoes. A shoe like the Adidas Adizero Adios 9 is a firm, low-cushion speed shoe, and our data lists its cushion as minimal and calls it too firm for long easy miles.

Test fit by walking in the shoe for 15 minutes in the store, with the socks you'll wear. Shoes that rub in the first hour will rub at the ninth. The [shoe lifespan guide](/rundown/how-long-do-running-shoes-last) explains when to replace them, and the same advice applies to walking miles.
