**Short answer:** Buy the Brooks Ghost 18 for one dependable shoe on every easy run, or the Hoka Clifton 11 if you want the softest ride and stand a lot. Both are $150.

## The specs are close except for stack and drop

Price and weight barely separate these two. The difference is how tall and how soft each one is.

- [Hoka Clifton 11](/shoes/hoka-clifton-11): $150, 9.7 oz (men's), 42 mm stack, 8 mm drop, maximum cushion.
- [Brooks Ghost 18](/shoes/brooks-ghost-18): $150, 9.8 oz (men's), 36 mm stack, 12 mm drop, moderate cushion.

In women's sizes the Clifton is 8 oz and the Ghost is 8.8 oz, so the weight gap is larger there. The Clifton 11 is newer in our data (July 2026) than the Ghost 18 (May 2026), and both are current models.

## The Ghost 18 is the default pick

The Ghost 18 uses the same nitrogen-infused DNA Loft v3 midsole as the Ghost 17. Our data lists a durable outsole, a fit that suits most feet and wide sizes. Its main weakness is a ride with little energy return.

Choose it if this is your first serious pair, or if you want one shoe that handles easy days and long runs. Our [guide to first running shoes](/rundown/best-running-shoes-for-beginners) covers what else to check before buying.

## The Clifton 11 is the softer, taller option

The Clifton 11 keeps the tall EVA platform and 8 mm drop of the [Clifton 10](/shoes/hoka-clifton-10). It has a rocker shape that rolls you forward, and our data says it works for walking and standing all day.

The cost is versatility. The foam is ordinary EVA at this price, and the shoe is not built for faster paces. Skip it if you want one shoe for tempo runs too.

## The 12 mm drop matters if you're switching

The Ghost's 12 mm drop is 4 mm higher than the Clifton's 8 mm, so the move between them is small. If you're coming from a 4 mm shoe, our [heel drop guide](/rundown/heel-drop-explained-4mm-vs-10mm) explains how to ramp up.

## Who each one is wrong for

- Ghost 18: wrong for runners who want a plush, cushioned feel underfoot.
- Clifton 11: wrong for runners who want a firmer, more connected ride or who mix in faster runs.
- Both: wrong for race day. Use the [shoe selector](/tools/shoe-selector) to filter for a speed shoe.

Either one fits as the daily half of a [two-shoe rotation](/rundown/shoe-rotation-two-pairs). Plan to replace it at the point covered in [how long running shoes last](/rundown/how-long-do-running-shoes-last).
