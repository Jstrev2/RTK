**Short answer:** Buy a shoe with proven wet-road grip. The Puma Velocity Nitro 5 is $140 and 8.1 oz, and our notes list "great grip in the wet".

## Wet grip is a rubber question

Our data records traction notes shoe by shoe, and the difference in the wet is clear. Three shoes are flagged as good in the wet, and four are flagged as weak. Check this before you buy, because wet roads are where weak rubber shows.

## Three shoes our notes flag as good in the wet

- [Puma Velocity Nitro 5](/shoes/puma-velocity-nitro-5): $140, 8.1 oz, 37 mm, 8 mm drop. "Great grip in the wet" and "light, versatile trainer".
- [New Balance Ellipse](/shoes/new-balance-ellipse): $145, 9.6 oz, 38 mm, 8 mm. "Good wet-road traction".
- [Craft Nordlite Tempo](/shoes/craft-nordlite-tempo): $180, 9.5 oz, 40 mm, 6 mm. "Excellent wet-road grip", with Vittoria-designed rubber. The fit runs narrow and slightly small.

## The Velocity Nitro 5 is the default

It is the lightest and cheapest of the three, and its 37 mm stack suits easy and steady runs. The New Balance Ellipse is \$5 more with a 38 mm stack and is heavier at 9.6 oz. The Nordlite Tempo costs $40 more and is heavier, so pick it only for the extra stack or its tempo-ready ride.

## Four shoes to skip on wet roads

- ASICS Gel-Cumulus 26: "outsole grip average in wet".
- Reebok Floatzig 2: "wet traction issues from open Zig outsole".
- Salomon Aero Blaze 3: "wet grip is mediocre in the forefoot".
- Topo Athletic Cyclone 3: "limited wet-surface traction".

These are fine on dry roads, and the Cumulus 26 and Floatzig 2 are budget-friendly at \$140 and \$120. The Salomon Aero Blaze 3 is \$140 and 7.9 oz, and the Cyclone 3 is a light speed shoe at \$150. If you already own one, run it on the [treadmill](/rundown/treadmill-running-shoes) or save it for sunny days.

## Who this is wrong for

Runners who only run in rain a few times a year. Keep your current daily trainer, since the difference only shows on wet pavement. Our data lists no waterproof shoe, so wet feet are likely in any of these.
