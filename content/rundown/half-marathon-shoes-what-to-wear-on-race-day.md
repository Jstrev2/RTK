**Short answer:** Race a half marathon in the ASICS Magic Speed 5: $160, 8.0 oz, a full carbon plate and a 39 mm stack. Your trainer is the better choice if you haven't run long in a plated shoe.

## A half sits between a trainer and a super shoe

A half marathon is long enough that cushion matters and short enough that a lighter shoe helps. Our data lists the Magic Speed 5 as "strong plated performance per dollar" and "lighter than most trainers".

Its cons are "less bouncy than flagship racers" and a narrow-ish performance fit.

## Four options from our data

- [ASICS Magic Speed 5](/shoes/asics-magic-speed-5): $160, 8.0 oz, 39 mm, 8 mm drop. Tough enough for repeated tempo sessions.
- [New Balance FuelCell Rebel v5](/shoes/new-balance-fuelcell-rebel-v5): $140, 7.8 oz, 35 mm, 6 mm drop. No plate, a "very light" ride, and average outsole durability.
- [Hoka Rocket X 3](/shoes/hoka-rocket-x-3): $250, 7.4 oz, 40 mm. Our notes call it a "strong half-to-full marathon pick" with an unusually stable ride.
- [Saucony Endorphin Speed 6](/shoes/saucony-endorphin-speed-6): $175, 8.2 oz. A nylon plate, and it trains well too.

## The Magic Speed 5 is the default

It is the cheapest full carbon plate in this list. It's also $15 under the Endorphin Speed 6, which is the better pick if you want a forgiving nylon plate. Our [Speed 6 versus Pro 5 guide](/rundown/saucony-endorphin-speed-6-vs-pro-5) compares the two Saucony plates.

## What to skip

- Shoes under about 30 mm of stack for a half. The Adidas Adizero Adios 9 is 27 mm and our data says its cushion is minimal for marathon distance.
- A shoe you haven't run in. Do at least two tempo runs or one long run in the shoe first.
- A new shoe in the week before the race. Pick it a month out.

## Who this is wrong for

- Runners new to the distance. A daily trainer is the safer pick, and the [daily trainer guide](/rundown/what-is-a-daily-trainer) explains why.
- Runners on a tight budget. The $95 ASICS Hyper Speed 6 is better at 5K and 10K, and our data says it isn't a marathon shoe for most.
- Runners with a $250 budget and a goal time. Plenty of super shoes are on the race-day list.
