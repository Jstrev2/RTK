**Short answer:** Buy the model and size you already wear if it worked. For a new shoe, measure your foot in the evening, check the return policy first and test the pair indoors on day one.

## Start from a shoe you know fits

The safest online purchase is the next version of a shoe you already run in. Updates between versions are usually small, as the [Pegasus 42 vs 41](/rundown/nike-pegasus-42-vs-41) comparison shows. Order the same size.

Switching brands carries the risk. Sizing varies between brands, and between models within a brand. Check our model page for notes on fit. The [Endorphin Speed 6](/shoes/saucony-endorphin-speed-6) listing, for example, says it fits slightly short and suggests sizing up.

## Measure in the evening, then check the size chart

Brand guides from ASICS, Brooks and REI all advise trying shoes on later in the day, when feet are larger. Measure the same way:

- Stand on a sheet of paper in the evening with your heel against a wall.
- Mark the end of your longest toe and measure to the wall in millimeters.
- Compare it to the brand's size chart, which lists foot length per size.
- Pick the size with at least half a thumb's width of extra room, as covered in our [sizing guide](/rundown/running-shoe-sizing-half-size-up).

## Read the return policy before the price

A cheap pair you can't return is not cheap if it doesn't fit. Check three things before checkout:

- Whether returns are free.
- How many days you have.
- Whether you can return shoes after you have worn them outside.

Many shops accept only unworn pairs, so wear the shoes indoors on a clean floor for the first test.

## Test on day one, then decide

Put the shoes on in the evening with the socks you run in. Check these:

- A thumb's width of room in front of the longest toe.
- A heel that doesn't lift when you walk fast.
- No pressure on the sides of the foot.

Jog in place or around a room for a few minutes. If anything rubs or pinches, send them back rather than waiting for them to break in.

## Who this is wrong for

Runners with an unusual foot shape, or with wide feet, will do better in a store for the first pair. Our [wide feet guide](/rundown/wide-feet-running-shoes) lists models that run roomy. After that first fit, ordering online works.
