**Short answer:** Run 10 to 20 minutes at easy pace, or 1 to 2 miles, with up to 4 short strides. Take the day off instead if you don't normally run the day before a hard day.

## A shakeout is 10 to 20 minutes

The goal is to loosen the legs and check the gear, not to train. These are our planning ranges, not a research result. We found no study that tests a run the day before a race.

- 5K or 10K: 15 to 20 minutes, up to 4 strides.
- Half marathon: 15 to 20 minutes, up to 4 strides.
- Marathon: 10 to 15 minutes, 2 to 4 strides or none.

Keep the pace slow enough to talk in full sentences. If you wear a watch, ignore the pace field.

## Strides are 20 seconds, not a workout

A stride is a smooth, fast surge of about 20 seconds, with 40 to 60 seconds of walking between. Run 2 to 4 of them near the end. They wake up the legs and take almost nothing out of them.

Skip strides if the course is hilly, the day is hot or you haven't done them before.

## Skip it when the day off helps

A day off is a fine choice. Take the day off if:

- You run 3 or fewer days a week.
- You're sore from a hard workout earlier in the week.
- Travel is the day's main job. Walking around the expo counts as activity.

The [weekly mileage guide](/rundown/how-many-miles-a-week-to-train-for-a-marathon) explains why easy days and days off both work. Pain is different. See a doctor or physical therapist before racing.

## Use the run to test the race kit

The shakeout is the last chance to catch a problem.

- Wear the race shoes, socks and shorts.
- Carry a gel and confirm it fits the pocket.
- Run to a short playlist from the [music tool](/tools/music) and check the volume.

Don't try new shoes, new socks or a new gel here. For the shoe choice, the [shoe selector](/tools/shoe-selector) narrows it before race week.

## What the rest of the day looks like

Keep the rest of the day low on your feet. Eat the carb-heavy meals from the [night-before guide](/rundown/what-to-eat-the-night-before-a-marathon), set out the kit and sleep. The race-day [pace calculator](/tools/pace-calculator) gives the splits to put on your wrist.
