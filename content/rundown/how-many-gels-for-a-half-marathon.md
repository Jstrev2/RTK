**Short answer:** Two gels cover most half marathons: one at 40 minutes and one at 70. Add a third if you'll be out past 2:15, and under 1:20 you can skip them.

## A half needs 30 to 60 grams of carbs an hour

Sports nutrition guidance puts races of one to two and a half hours at 30 to 60 grams of carbs an hour. Under an hour the benefit is small. A half marathon sits in that middle band for almost everyone, so it needs far less than a marathon plan.

Most gels in our [gel table](/fuel) carry 22 to 25 grams. Two 25 g gels in a 1:45 race is 50 grams, about 29 grams an hour. That's the low end of the band, and enough for most runners at that distance.

## Gels by finish time

- Under 1:20: none needed. Your stored carbs cover the race. Take one at 40 minutes if it helps you mentally.
- 1:20 to 1:45: two gels, at about 40 and 70 minutes.
- 1:45 to 2:15: two or three, at 40, 70 and 100 minutes. Take the third if you feel flat at 100.
- Over 2:15: one every 40 to 45 minutes, which is three or four.

Each of these finish times has a full plan with miles and a packing list: [1:45](/fuel/plan/half-marathon/1-45), [2:00](/fuel/plan/half-marathon/2-00) and [2:15](/fuel/plan/half-marathon/2-15).

## Choose the gel by caffeine, sodium and thickness

- Caffeine: the [caffeinated gels](/fuel/best/caffeinated-gels) in our table range from 25 to 100 mg. If you don't drink coffee, take half a dose or skip it.
- Sodium: our gels carry 0 to 245 mg each. A salty gel helps on a hot day and makes little difference on a cool one.
- Thickness: the 40 g Maurten Gel 160 is a lot to swallow at once. Thinner gels go down easier. Take any gel with a few sips of water.

For more carbs per packet, the [30 g gels](/fuel/best/30g-carb-gels) let two packets do the work of three.

## Try both gels on a long run first

Eat your usual breakfast two to three hours before the start. Use only a gel you've taken in training: race day is a bad time to learn that a flavor or brand upsets your stomach.

If gels have given you trouble before, [training your gut for race fueling](/rundown/train-your-gut-race-fueling) shows how to build up. Carry a spare in your pocket either way. If you feel strong at 100 minutes, you won't need it.
