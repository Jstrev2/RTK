**Short answer:** The Saucony Ride 19 is the closest alternative to the Nike Pegasus 42: $145, 9.3 oz and an 8 mm drop. For less money, the Nike Winflo 12 is $110.

## The Pegasus 42 is the benchmark

The [Pegasus 42](/shoes/nike-pegasus-42) is $145, 9.8 oz, with a 37 mm stack and a 10 mm drop. It is a durable, moderately cushioned trainer with an accommodating fit. Our data lists its weaknesses as an incremental update and middle-of-the-pack weight.

Every shoe below is a neutral daily trainer within $5 of it, unless noted.

## The Saucony Ride 19 is the closest match

The [Ride 19](/shoes/saucony-ride-19) is $145 and 9.3 oz with a 35 mm stack and an 8 mm drop. It is the same kind of shoe: balanced cushion, durable outsole and upper, one pair for most of a training week.

It is wrong for runners who like the Pegasus's 10 mm drop and want it kept.

## Other neutral trainers that fit the same job

- [Mizuno Wave Rider 30](/shoes/mizuno-wave-rider-30): $145, 9.8 oz, 38 mm stack, 12 mm drop. Structured and built to last, with a firmer feel.
- [ASICS Gel-Cumulus 28](/shoes/asics-gel-cumulus-28): $145, 9.2 oz, 38 mm stack, 8 mm drop. Light for its cushion.
- Brooks Ghost 18: $150, 9.8 oz, 36 mm stack, 12 mm drop. The safest fit for most feet.
- [Puma Velocity Nitro 5](/shoes/puma-velocity-nitro-5): $140, 8.1 oz, 37 mm stack, 8 mm drop. The lightest of the group, with good wet grip.
- New Balance Fresh Foam X 880 v15: $140, 10.4 oz, 36 mm stack, 8 mm drop. Heavier, with width options.

## Spend less with the Winflo 12 or the last model

The [Winflo 12](/shoes/nike-winflo-12) is $110 and 9.7 oz with a 36 mm stack and a 10 mm drop, the same drop as the Pegasus. It is firmer than Nike's premium foams and is built for easy running, not workouts.

The Pegasus 41 is listed at $140 and 9.9 oz. Our [Pegasus 42 vs 41 guide](/rundown/nike-pegasus-42-vs-41) covers what changed between them.

## Who should skip all of these

Runners who want more cushion should look at the max-cushion tier instead, such as the Nike Vomero 18 at $150 and 46 mm. Runners who want a lighter shoe for workouts should read our [lightweight trainers guide](/rundown/lightweight-trainers-for-faster-days).
