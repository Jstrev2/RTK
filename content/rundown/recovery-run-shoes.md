**Short answer:** Pick a soft, stable shoe with a 37 mm or taller stack for recovery days. The Saucony Triumph 24 is $170 and 9.7 oz, and our data lists it as "excellent for long easy miles".

## Recovery days need cushion, not speed

Recovery runs are short and easy, so weight and pop don't matter. Our data lists the shoes built for it as "too soft and tall for fast workouts", which is the point. A dedicated pair also keeps your fast shoes for fast days, as the [rotation guide](/rundown/shoe-rotation-two-pairs) explains.

## Five soft options from our data

- [Saucony Triumph 24](/shoes/saucony-triumph-24): $170, 9.7 oz, 37 mm, 10 mm drop. "Plush, energetic PEBA cushioning".
- [Brooks Ghost Max 4](/shoes/brooks-ghost-max-4): $160, 10.3 oz, 39 mm, 6 mm. "Maximum protection for easy miles" with a stable base.
- ASICS Gel-Nimbus 29: $170, 9.8 oz, 43 mm, 8 mm. Plush, and lighter than the Nimbus 28.
- Hoka Bondi 9: $170, 10.6 oz, 43 mm, 5 mm. "Very protective for recovery days", and sluggish above easy pace.
- Saucony Procyon Max: $120, 10.7 oz, 38 mm, 6 mm. The budget pick, with "lots of cushion for $120".

## The Triumph 24 is the default

It is the lightest of the five at 9.7 oz and listed for recovery runs. Its limit is price: our data calls it expensive and too soft for fast workouts.

Buy the Procyon Max instead if you want a second pair for $50 less. It is heavier at 10.7 oz and "not lively at faster paces", which does not matter on a recovery day.

## The Gel-Excite 11 is the cheapest at $90

The [ASICS Gel-Excite 11](/shoes/asics-gel-excite-11) is $90, 10.2 oz, 38 mm, and our notes say it is "comfortable for walking/easy miles". It also lists "heavy and basic foam" and "not built for serious mileage", so treat it as a short-run shoe.

## Who this advice is wrong for

- Runners who run 3 days a week. One daily trainer is enough, so start with the [daily trainer guide](/rundown/what-is-a-daily-trainer).
- Forefoot strikers who dislike a 10 mm drop. The Triumph 24 is 10 mm, and our Triumph 23 notes say the drop "won't suit forefoot strikers". The Ghost Max 4 at 6 mm is the alternative.
- Heavier runners who want more support. The [heavier runners guide](/rundown/running-shoes-for-heavier-runners) fits them better.
