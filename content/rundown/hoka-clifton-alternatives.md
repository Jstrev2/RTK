**Short answer:** The ASICS Novablast 6 is the best Hoka Clifton alternative: same $150 price, 9.0 oz against 9.7 oz, and a 41 mm stack. For a roomier fit, the Topo Phantom 4 has a wide toe box.

## The Clifton 11 is the benchmark

The [Clifton 11](/shoes/hoka-clifton-11) is $150 and 9.7 oz with a 42 mm stack and an 8 mm drop. Our data lists it as plush and rockered, good for walking and standing, with ordinary EVA foam and no versatility at faster paces. Our [Clifton 11 vs Ghost 18 guide](/rundown/hoka-clifton-11-vs-brooks-ghost-18) compares it to its main rival.

## The Novablast 6 is lighter and bouncier

The [Novablast 6](/shoes/asics-novablast-6) keeps a tall 41 mm stack and the same 8 mm drop at $150. It weighs 9.0 oz and is listed as light for its cushion with an energetic ride.

It is not the most stable platform. Runners who want a planted feel should choose another shoe from this list.

## More cushion, softer foam or a lower price

- [Nike Vomero 18](/shoes/nike-vomero-18): $150, 10.5 oz, 46 mm stack, 10 mm drop. Taller and softer, heavier.
- Saucony Triumph 24: $170, 9.7 oz, 37 mm stack, 10 mm drop. Plush PEBA foam, a higher price.
- ASICS Gel-Nimbus 28: $165, 10.6 oz, 43 mm stack, 8 mm drop. Plush and durable, a heavy shoe.
- [Saucony Procyon Max](/shoes/saucony-procyon-max): $120, 10.7 oz, 38 mm stack, 6 mm drop. Max cushion on a budget, stable and broad.

## Wider fit or a different drop

The [Topo Athletic Phantom 4](/shoes/topo-athletic-phantom-4) is $150 and 9.5 oz with a 35 mm stack, a 5 mm drop and a wide, anatomical toe box. Its ZipFoam is less lively than newer foams.

The Hoka Arahi 9 is $150 and 9.7 oz with an 8 mm drop and a roomier forefoot than the Arahi 8. It adds light stability from a J-Frame guide. Our [wide feet guide](/rundown/wide-feet-running-shoes) lists more roomy options.

## Who should stay with the Clifton

Runners who stand or walk all day in their shoes should keep the Clifton. The rocker and 42 mm stack are built for that, and the alternatives above are tuned more for running.

If the Clifton feels too soft and slow for tempo days, add a lighter second pair rather than replacing it. The [shoe rotation guide](/rundown/shoe-rotation-two-pairs) covers how to pair them.
