**Short answer:** Buy a light, firm racing flat for a 5K or 10K. The ASICS Hyper Speed 6 is $95 and 6.6 oz, and our data calls it the cheapest true speed shoe in its lineup.

## Short races need weight savings, not stack

A 5K or 10K is over before cushion matters, so the shoes built for it are low and light. All five below weigh 7.1 oz or less and have a stack of 33 mm or lower. Their usual trade is a firm ride that gets harsh past 10K, which is why our notes flag most of them as wrong for longer races.

## Five options from our data

- [ASICS Hyper Speed 6](/shoes/asics-hyper-speed-6): $95, 6.6 oz, 33 mm, 5 mm drop. "Light, flexible, natural-feeling", with "modest cushioning and bounce".
- [Adidas Adizero Adios 9](/shoes/adidas-adizero-adios-9): $130, 7.1 oz, 27 mm, 8 mm. "Nimble, close-to-the-ground ride" and a durable Continental rubber outsole.
- [Topo Athletic Cyclone 3](/shoes/topo-athletic-cyclone-3): $150, 6.9 oz, 28 mm, 5 mm. Roomy toe box, but "limited wet-surface traction".
- [Nike Streakfly 2](/shoes/nike-streakfly-2): $170, 6.2 oz, 32 mm, 6 mm. A midfoot plate and the lightest of the five, with "too little cushion for long races".
- [Adidas Adizero Takumi Sen 11](/shoes/adidas-adizero-takumi-sen-11): $180, 6.5 oz, 33 mm, 6 mm. Grippy outsole, narrow racing fit, "too firm and low for most runners over 10K".

## The Hyper Speed 6 is the default

It costs $35 less than the next cheapest option and is "durable enough to train in", so it covers intervals as well as race day. Plateless shoes also take less getting used to than a carbon plate.

Pick the Takumi Sen 11 instead if you land on your midfoot or forefoot and want the most aggressive fit. Pick the Cyclone 3 if other racing flats pinch your toes and the race will be dry.

## Who a racing flat is wrong for

- Runners whose 10K takes over an hour. The firm ride gets harder to tolerate the longer you're on it, so a lightweight trainer is the safer fit.
- Anyone racing a half marathon or longer. The [half marathon shoe guide](/rundown/half-marathon-shoes-what-to-wear-on-race-day) covers that.
- Runners who want one shoe for everything. The Saucony Endorphin Speed 6 is $175 and 8.2 oz, with a 38 mm stack.
