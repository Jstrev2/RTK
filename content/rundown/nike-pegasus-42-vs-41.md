**Short answer:** Buy whichever Pegasus costs less. The 42 is $145 against $140 for the 41, with the same 37 mm stack, 10 mm drop and ReactX midsole.

## The numbers differ by less than an ounce

- [Nike Pegasus 42](/shoes/nike-pegasus-42): $145, 9.8 oz (men's), 8.7 oz (women's), 37 mm stack, 10 mm drop. Released May 2026.
- [Nike Pegasus 41](/shoes/nike-pegasus-41): $140, 9.9 oz (men's), 8.6 oz (women's), 37 mm stack, 10 mm drop. Released June 2024.

The 42 is 0.1 oz lighter in men's sizes and 0.1 oz heavier in women's. That is not a difference you will feel.

## What changed is the upper

Our data says the 42 keeps the ReactX midsole and dual Air Zoom units of the 41. Nike changed the upper and made small fit tweaks. Our data lists the 42 as an incremental update, so the ride should feel the same.

The 41 moved to full-length ReactX foam, a bouncier ride than the Pegasus 40. If you liked the 41, the 42 will not surprise you. Our [comparison page](/shoes/compare/nike-pegasus-41-vs-nike-pegasus-42) lists every spec side by side.

## Buy the 41 on sale, the 42 at list price

Last year's model is where the savings are, because the changes between versions are small. If the 41 is marked down by $20 or more, it is the value pick. If the two are within a few dollars, take the 42 for the newer upper and a fresher design.

If you wear a 41 now, there is no reason to switch before it wears out. Our guide to [how long running shoes last](/rundown/how-long-do-running-shoes-last) gives the signs to watch.

## Who the Pegasus is wrong for

- Runners who want maximum softness: the Hoka Clifton line is plusher. See [Clifton 11 vs Ghost 18](/rundown/hoka-clifton-11-vs-brooks-ghost-18).
- Runners who want a very light ride: 9.8 oz is middle of the pack.
- Runners who want speed work shoes: the Pegasus is a trainer, not a tempo shoe.

For everyone else, it is a durable, moderate-cushion trainer that fits most feet. Use the [shoe selector](/tools/shoe-selector) to check it against your own foot and pace.
