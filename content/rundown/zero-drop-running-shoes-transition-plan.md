**Short answer:** Spend 6 to 8 weeks moving into zero drop, one easy run a week at first. Start in a cushioned shoe like the Altra Torin 9 ($150, 10.1 oz, 30 mm stack), not a thin one.

## Zero drop moves work to your calves

In a zero-drop shoe the heel and forefoot sit at the same height. Most trainers sit 8 to 12 mm higher at the heel, so the calf and Achilles stretch further on every step. Our data flags the same cost on several models: the Altra Escalante 4 lists "zero drop requires calf adaptation" and the Paradigm 8 says "zero drop needs a calf transition".

The research on drop and injury is thin, as the [heel drop guide](/rundown/heel-drop-explained-4mm-vs-10mm) covers. Treat the ramp below as caution, not a proven protocol.

## Start in a cushioned zero-drop shoe

A thin shoe adds two changes at once: less drop and less foam. These are the zero-drop shoes in our data worth starting in:

- [Altra Torin 9](/shoes/altra-torin-9): $150, 10.1 oz, 30 mm. Our notes call it an "easy transition shoe into zero drop".
- [Topo Magnifly 5](/shoes/topo-athletic-magnifly-5): $140, 9.6 oz, 30 mm. Cushioned, with a wide toe box.
- [Altra Escalante 5](/shoes/altra-escalante-5): $130, 9.2 oz, 24 mm. Thin, "too thin for high mileage", so a later purchase.

## Ramp over 6 to 8 weeks

- Weeks 1 and 2: one easy run a week of 20 to 30 minutes in the zero-drop pair. Everything else stays in your old shoes.
- Weeks 3 and 4: two easy runs a week in them, still under 45 minutes.
- Weeks 5 and 6: add one longer easy run, and keep speed work in your old shoes.
- Weeks 7 and 8: most easy miles in the new pair if your calves feel normal the next morning.

Repeat a step if tightness hasn't faded by the next run. If calf or Achilles pain lingers, see a doctor or physical therapist.

## Try 4 mm first if the jump looks big

Runners coming from 10 or 12 mm shoes can step down in two stages. The [Altra Experience Flow 3](/shoes/altra-experience-flow-3) is $145, 9.0 oz, 32 mm with a 4 mm drop and the same wide toe box. Our [low-drop shoe list](/shoes/best/low-drop-running-shoes) has more.

## Who should skip zero drop

- Runners with calves or Achilles tendons that already flare up in current shoes.
- Anyone training for a race inside 8 weeks. Change shoes after the race, not before.
- Runners who want one shoe for racing. The zero-drop [Altra Vanish Pulse](/shoes/altra-vanish-pulse) is 6.9 oz with a 22 mm stack, and our data limits it to shorter efforts.
