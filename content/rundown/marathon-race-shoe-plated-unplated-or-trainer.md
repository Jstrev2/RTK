**Short answer:** Race the marathon in a plated shoe once you've run two long runs in it, such as the $175 Saucony Endorphin Speed 6. Without that practice, wear your daily trainer.

## What a plate was measured to do

Hoogkamer and colleagues (2018, Sports Medicine) put 18 male runners on a treadmill in a Nike prototype and two other marathon shoes. Energetic cost was about 4% lower in the prototype. Each runner had run a 10K in under 31 minutes, so the figure is for very fast men.

Our [carbon plate guide](/rundown/carbon-plate-shoes-for-non-elite-runners) covers what that means at slower paces. We don't quote a saving for them.

## Three choices from our data

- Your trainer. The Nike Zoom Fly 6 is a carbon super trainer at $170, 9.4 oz and 42 mm. It is "noticeably heavier than race-day supershoes".
- Plated, mid-price. The [Endorphin Speed 6](/shoes/saucony-endorphin-speed-6) has a nylon plate, 38 mm stack and 8 mm drop. Our data lists it as durable enough for regular training and says it fits slightly short.
- Super shoe. The [Vaporfly 4](/shoes/nike-vaporfly-4) is $260 and 6.0 oz. The Adidas Adizero Adios Pro 5 is $250 and 7.6 oz, built for full-effort half marathons and marathons.

## The Endorphin Speed 6 is the default

It costs $75 to $85 less than the super shoes and gives up 1.6 oz to 2.2 oz. It is also firm enough to train in, so the two practice long runs are cheap to do.

Buy the Zoom Fly 6 instead if you want a single plated shoe for long runs and race day. The [super trainers guide](/rundown/super-trainers-explained) covers that category.

## Break it in before race day

Run at least two long runs in the shoe, one of them with a stretch at goal pace. Check for hot spots and toe room after mile 15, not mile 3. Taper runs don't count, because tired legs are what you are testing.

Plan fueling too: the [marathon gel guide](/rundown/how-many-gels-for-a-marathon) has the numbers.

## Who this advice is wrong for

- Wide feet. The Vaporfly 4 is narrow, and the Endorphin Speed 6 is standard width.
- First-time marathoners running to finish. Your trainer is the safest pick, as the [first racing shoe guide](/rundown/how-to-choose-your-first-racing-shoe) explains.
- Runners with a time goal who want the lightest option. Compare the [Vaporfly 4 and Alphafly 4](/rundown/nike-vaporfly-4-vs-alphafly-4).
