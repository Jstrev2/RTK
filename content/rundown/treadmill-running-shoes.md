**Short answer:** You don't need a treadmill-specific shoe. Wear your regular daily trainer, and save any shoe with weak wet grip for the belt, where grip never gets tested by rain.

## Our data has no treadmill category

We don't list a shoe as built for treadmills, and no gear review in our data claims one runs better on a belt. The only shoe whose notes mention treadmill miles is the [Hoka Kawana 2](/shoes/hoka-kawana-2). It is described as a run-to-gym crossover, a firmer, flatter, more stable shoe for treadmill miles, lifting and errands. It is $140, 10.4 oz, 36 mm, 5 mm drop.

## Indoors is the place for shoes that slip in the rain

Our notes flag several shoes for weak wet grip. A belt is dry, so that weakness costs you nothing there:

- ASICS Gel-Cumulus 26: $140, 9.0 oz, 38.5 mm. "Outsole grip average in wet".
- [Reebok Floatzig 2](/shoes/reebok-floatzig-2): $120, 10.3 oz, 33 mm. "Wet traction issues from open Zig outsole", and it is "great for easy and recovery miles".
- Topo Athletic Cyclone 3: $150, 6.9 oz, 28 mm. "Limited wet-surface traction", and a good light shoe for treadmill intervals.

Using them indoors also saves your outdoor shoes for the road. It also means the grip flaw that rules them out for rain, covered in the [rain shoes guide](/rundown/running-shoes-for-rain), never comes up. A belt does not get wet, so only the cushion and weight of the shoe matter.

## The Floatzig 2 is the cheap treadmill pair

At $120 it is the cheapest of the three, and its listed use is easy miles. A second pair kept at the gym also means no shoe change in the car.

Skip it if your treadmill runs are fast. For those, the Cyclone 3 is the lighter pick and the [lightweight trainers guide](/rundown/lightweight-trainers-for-faster-days) has more.

## Who this is wrong for

- Runners with one pair. Wear your daily trainer, such as the [Brooks Ghost 18](/shoes/brooks-ghost-18) at $150 and 9.8 oz.
- Runners who also lift. The Kawana 2 is the one to look at.
- Anyone setting a treadmill pace. Read the [1% incline guide](/rundown/treadmill-pace-vs-outdoor-pace-1-percent-incline) first; shoes don't change that number.
