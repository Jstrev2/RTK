**Short answer:** Buy the Hoka Mach 7 ($145, 8.3 oz, 37 mm stack, 5 mm drop) as the light shoe for tempo runs and intervals. It also works for easy miles, so it can be your only trainer.

## The Mach 7 is the default

The [Hoka Mach 7](/shoes/hoka-mach-7) is built for easy pace through tempo, with a 37 mm stack that protects longer efforts. Our notes list it as "versatile from easy pace to tempo" and "light for a full daily trainer".

Its limit: our data says it is not for max-cushion lovers, so it is a poor recovery shoe. The Mach 6 at $140 and 8.2 oz had thin outsole rubber, and the Mach 7 adds slightly improved coverage.

## Four others with different trade-offs

- [Saucony Kinvara 16](/shoes/saucony-kinvara-16): $130, 7.0 oz, 29 mm, 4 mm drop. The lightest here, but "low energy return from dated foam" and a narrow fit.
- [New Balance FuelCell Rebel v5](/shoes/new-balance-fuelcell-rebel-v5): $140, 7.8 oz, 35 mm, 6 mm. "Very light with lively bounce", and rated down for average outsole durability.
- Brooks Launch 11: $120, 7.7 oz, 34 mm, 8 mm. The budget pick, with "basic firm foam" and sparse cushioning for long runs.
- Skechers Aero Spark: $120, 7.6 oz, 33 mm, 6 mm. "Light and lively for the price", with thin outsole rubber.

## Pick by what you do on the fast day

For 400 m to mile repeats, the lighter and lower shoes fit: the Kinvara 16 at 29 mm or the Aero Spark at 33 mm. For a 40 to 60 minute tempo, the Mach 7 at 37 mm or the Rebel v5 at 35 mm give more foam under you.

Race-day pace is a separate question. Plated shoes are covered in the [carbon plate guide](/rundown/carbon-plate-shoes-for-non-elite-runners).

## Light shoes wear faster

Several of the shoes above list thin outsole rubber or average durability in our data. Expect fewer miles from them than from a heavier daily trainer. The signs to watch are in our [shoe lifespan guide](/rundown/how-long-do-running-shoes-last).

## Who this advice is wrong for

- Runners who need stability. These five are neutral and light.
- Heavier runners. Our data says the lightweight Aero Blaze 3 has a "midsole that compresses under heavier runners".
- Anyone doing all their runs at easy pace. A [daily trainer](/rundown/what-is-a-daily-trainer) is the better single shoe.
