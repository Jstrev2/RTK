**Short answer:** High arches don't call for a special shoe. Pick a cushioned neutral trainer that feels comfortable, such as the Hoka Clifton 11 at $150, 9.7 oz and 42 mm.

## No study we can name ties arch height to a shoe type

Our shoe data has no arch field, so we don't claim to match shoes to arch height. The study we can cite is Nielsen and colleagues (2014, British Journal of Sports Medicine): 927 novice runners in one neutral shoe, followed for a year. Runners with supinated feet, the high-arch posture, had no significant difference in injury risk from neutral feet after 250 km.

It is one study of beginners. It supports choosing by comfort, which Nigg and colleagues (2015, same journal) proposed as a "comfort filter".

## Start with a neutral trainer with plenty of cushion

Cushion is the one spec you can check in our data. These are all neutral, maximum-cushion trainers:

- [Hoka Clifton 11](/shoes/hoka-clifton-11): $150, 9.7 oz, 42 mm, 8 mm drop. "Familiar plush Clifton ride".
- [Brooks Glycerin 23](/shoes/brooks-glycerin-23): $165, 10.1 oz, 38 mm, 10 mm drop. "Plush, premium ride".
- [ASICS Gel-Nimbus 29](/shoes/asics-gel-nimbus-29): $170, 9.8 oz, 43 mm, 8 mm drop. Too soft for fast workouts.
- [Skechers Aero Burst](/shoes/skechers-aero-burst): $130, 9.0 oz, 38 mm, 6 mm drop. Soft and cheap, with an upper that runs warm.

## The Clifton 11 is the default

It is $15 under the Glycerin 23 and lighter. Our data lists "smooth rockered transitions" as a strength and says it isn't versatile for faster paces.

Buy the Aero Burst instead if $130 is the ceiling. Our data lists limited run-specialty distribution, so check the return policy before you order.

## Check three things on day one

- Toe room. Your longest toe should have a thumb's width in front of it.
- Heel hold. The heel shouldn't lift on a jog.
- Midfoot pressure. A tight spot over the top of the foot means the lacing or the shoe is wrong.

The [sizing guide](/rundown/running-shoe-sizing-half-size-up) explains the half size up.

## Who this is wrong for

- Runners who like ground feel. A tall, soft stack mutes it, and the [max cushion guide](/rundown/max-cushion-running-shoes-who-they-help) covers the trade.
- Runners who feel unsteady in soft shoes. Try a mild stability trainer such as the [Brooks Ghost Max 4](/shoes/brooks-ghost-max-4).
- Anyone with foot pain. See a doctor or physical therapist.
